# Feature Catalog

The `/api/v1/public/features` endpoint returns a list of high‑level features available in Bitcoin Bastion. Each entry describes a capability and its current status (beta, production, planned). This catalog is public and does not require Proof‑of‑Access.

Current categories include:

- **Trace** – advisory risk assessments for transactions and addresses.
- **Citadel** – wallet resilience analysis, inheritance plan verification and risk scoring.
- **Treasury** – policy checks and withdrawal request evaluation.
- **Register** – registration and evidence for deployments and providers.
- **Observability** – health and status metrics for providers and jobs.
- **Access** – Proof‑of‑Access plan codes and subscription entitlements.

Additional features (e.g. delegated passes, offline validity packs, post‑quantum signatures) may be listed as `planned` in this catalog before full implementation.
