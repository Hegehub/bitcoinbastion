# Support Boundaries

Bitcoin Bastion provides advisory, evidence-oriented analysis and operational tooling baselines.
It does not custody funds, sign transactions, or provide legal/compliance certification.
Manual review and external controls remain required.

Bitcoin consensus remains with Bitcoin nodes and the Bitcoin protocol.

## Access recovery support boundary

Support cannot ask for a Bitcoin seed, Bitcoin private key, xprv, WIF key, wallet file, raw Bastion Access Pass, raw session token, or Bastion Recovery Seed in plaintext. Support cannot unilaterally recover Pro, Business, or Enterprise access. Recovery follows quorum, cooldown, issuer-policy, and audit rules. Enterprise recovery follows the customer policy and recovery ceremony requirements.
