# Support Boundaries

Bitcoin Bastion provides advisory analysis, recovery tooling and operational dashboards. It is not a wallet, does not custody funds, does not sign transactions, and cannot bypass security policies.

## What support can do

- Provide guidance on using the public and Proof‑of‑Access APIs.
- Help operators interpret risk scores, provider health, evidence packets and recovery status.
- Assist with environment configuration and deployment troubleshooting.
- Explain how to set up Bastion Recovery Seed and the recovery quorum for your plan.

## What support cannot do

- **Ask for your Bitcoin seed, Bitcoin private key or recovery phrase.** Bastion support will never request or store these secrets. Keep your wallet seed separate and never enter it into Bastion.
- **Ask for your Bastion Recovery Seed or device private keys.** Support cannot see or reset your recovery phrase; recovery is user‑controlled.
- **Unilaterally restore Pro, Business or Enterprise access.** High‑tier recovery requires the configured quorum of factors and may be subject to a cooldown. Support cannot bypass these controls.
- **Override policy decisions.** Access is determined by the policy engine, certificate status, entitlements and revocation registry. Support cannot grant additional scopes or bypass plan limits.
- **Sign transactions or custody funds.** Bastion is non‑custodial. Support cannot facilitate withdrawals, send Bitcoin or sign spending transactions.
- **Provide legal, financial or compliance certification.** Bitcoin Bastion is an informational and operational platform; it does not replace professional advice.

Recovery must never be easier than login. If you lose your Bastion Recovery Seed and all devices, recovery may be impossible for high‑tier plans. Please keep your seed safe and follow the recovery setup instructions.
