# Bastion Market Time Machine

The Bastion Market Time Machine is the Bitcoin-first intelligence layer for news ingestion, source health, canonical news events, BTC market data, candles, news scoring, impact windows, candle attribution, historical similarity, Market Memory, and operator-governed signal publication.

## Governance update

Task 36 adds candidate signals, publishing policies, operator review, frontend-ready public signal DTOs, delivery logs, and bounded observability metrics. Auto-publish is disabled by default because Bitcoin Bastion prioritizes evidence, operator control, and no-causation safety over automated market claims.

Signals are informational only. Correlation is not causation. Outputs are not financial advice.
